Summary

Italy's 2026 hyper-depreciation (iperammortamento) lets companies deduct an increased cost for investments in new "Industry 4.0" capital goods made from 1 January 2026 to 30 September 2028. The increase is 180% up to €2.5 million of investment, 100% from €2.5 to €10 million and 50% from €10 to €20 million.

For a machine tool the key point is interconnection: the machine must exchange data with the company's information systems, and an expert must certify it in a sworn technical report, mandatory for any amount. Without interconnection the incentive does not apply, even if the machine has every other feature.

The benefit starts in the year the machine is actually interconnected, not when it is delivered. Connecting it to the ERP straight away avoids delaying the benefit and the application deadlines.

What "interconnected" means

  • Two-way exchange: the machine receives instructions from the company system and returns production data. A link that only "reads" the machine is not enough.
  • Documented, recognised protocols such as OPC UA, Modbus or MQTT.
  • Unique identification of the machine on the company network, for example a dedicated IP address.
  • Useful for production traceability.

Evidence for the expert

  • Timeline: calls received from the ERP with their result, state changes, running program, connection lost and restored, alarms, counted parts, with the machine values at any moment.
  • Audit package: exports the chosen period (up to 31 days) with the timeline in CSV, machine value changes and a summary, with operator names replaced by numbers.
  • Activity log exportable to CSV, work order history and period reports.
The detailed timeline is kept for 35 days and weekly packages for 8 weeks: download the audit package for the inspection period and keep it with the application documents.

Timing and upkeep

  • The application goes through the GSE with several notices: a preliminary one, a confirmation with the down payment and the completion notice with the technical report and accounting certification, by 15 November 2028, followed by periodic notices (20 January and 30 June each year). Delays can forfeit the benefit.
  • The benefit starts in the period the machine is actually interconnected.
  • Interconnection must be maintained over the years: if it is lost, the remaining benefit may be revoked. RTIndustry keeps the link active and the timeline flags interruptions with their duration.
In development: an interconnection dossier generated by RTIndustry for each machine, with the date of first interconnection, the exchanges with the ERP and the continuity of the link, designed for the expert's work.

FAQ

Is installing RTIndustry enough to obtain the hyper-depreciation?

No. You need a new asset with the Industry 4.0 features required by law, the sworn technical report and the notices to the GSE. RTIndustry handles the interconnection and keeps the evidence to show the expert.

Does it need the cloud or a permanent internet connection?

No. RTIndustry runs on the company network with a built-in database. The internet is only used for periodic licence checks, and the software keeps working offline.

Our ERP has no ready-made integration

RTIndustry offers three equivalent channels: REST API, database exchange table and MQTT. The ERP vendor will find everything in the ERP and MES integration guide.
