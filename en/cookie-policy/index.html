Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 February 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your browser, where they are stored so that they can be sent back to the same websites on your next visit. Cookies are used for various purposes: authentication, session monitoring, storing preferences and statistical analysis.

2. Cookies used by this website

This website does not use its own cookies or third-party profiling cookies. No analytics tools (Google Analytics or similar), tracking pixels, social widgets or other tools that set cookies are installed.

Browsing this website does not result in RTIndustry installing cookies on the user's device.

3. GitHub Pages technical cookies

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may set strictly technical cookies needed for the infrastructure to work (CDN, load balancing, DDoS protection). These cookies:

  • Are strictly necessary for the hosting service to work
  • Do not collect personally identifiable information
  • Are not used for profiling or marketing purposes
  • Do not require the user's consent under Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, as they are essential for providing the service

4. How to manage cookies

Even though this website does not set cookies, users can manage their cookie preferences at any time through their browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies / Site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data
  • Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data

Disabling technical cookies may affect the correct operation of some websites.

5. Changes to this policy

RTIndustry reserves the right to update this Cookie Policy at any time, for example if new features requiring the use of cookies are introduced. Any changes will be published on this page, with the revision date at the top updated.

6. Contact

For any questions about this Cookie Policy, you can contact us at assistenza@rtindustry.it.

For full information on the processing of personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
