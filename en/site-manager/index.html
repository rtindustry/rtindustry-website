Features

Licenses, installations and events

Each RTIndustry workstation verifies its license with the Site Manager and periodically sends a status summary. Work on the shop floor does not depend on the connection: if it is missing, RTIndustry keeps collecting data and retries by itself.

Licenses and seats

Keys in the XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX format with expiry date, status (active, suspended, revoked) and number of seats, that is of PCs that can use them.

Installations

Online and offline status of every workstation, program version and last contact. Workstations register themselves on activation.

Status and alerts

Periodic summary of production and PC health (for example disk space), with alerts for workstations that stop reporting.

Customer area

Customers see their installations, free a seat by deactivating an old PC and download the latest installer, without calling support.

How it works

From the workstation to the panel

RTIndustry workstation

On the shop-floor PC, connected to the CNC or PLC. It activates the license with the key.

Site Manager

Verifies the license, registers the installation and receives a status summary every 5 minutes. It also works through the company proxy.

Who manages

System integrators and resellers follow their own installations; the customer uses the customer area.

Access

Everyone sees only what concerns them

Administrator

  • All licenses and installations
  • Management of users and roles
  • Assignment of licenses to resellers

Reseller

  • Only the licenses and installations assigned
  • Status and events of their own customers
  • No access to other resellers' data

Viewer

  • Read-only access
  • Licenses, installations and events
  • No changes
